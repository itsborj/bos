Privacy Policy

Klaroha helps sales-led businesses capture, qualify, route, and follow up on buyer intent. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and how users can request access, deletion, or disconnection.

Effective date: May 26, 2026

Contact: privacy@klaroha.com

1. Who we are

Klaroha provides marketing operating system software and related services for businesses that manage leads, campaigns, content, analytics, and customer conversations across multiple channels.

For most customer data, Klaroha acts as a service provider or processor on behalf of the business using Klaroha. For our own website, account, billing, and product analytics data, Klaroha acts as the controller.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: name, email, business name, role, contact details, and login or authentication information.
  • Workspace and brand information: brand profiles, campaign briefs, audience notes, product or service details, creative assets, approvals, and operating preferences.
  • Lead and conversation information: information submitted through forms, messages, booking flows, campaign replies, and related qualification data that our customers choose to process in Klaroha.
  • Integration data: connection status, account identifiers, page or channel identifiers, content metadata, campaign performance, and other data made available through authorized third-party integrations.
  • Usage and device data: log data, browser type, IP address, pages viewed, feature usage, error reports, and security events.

3. TikTok and social platform data

If a customer connects TikTok or another social platform to Klaroha, we access only the data and permissions the customer authorizes and only for the product functions requested by the customer.

Depending on the approved permissions and customer configuration, this may include account or profile identifiers, content metadata, post or video publishing status, engagement or performance metrics, and media assets selected by the customer for publishing or analysis.

Klaroha does not sell TikTok user data, does not use TikTok data to build unrelated consumer profiles, and does not share TikTok data with third parties except as needed to operate Klaroha, comply with law, protect security, or follow the customer's instructions.

4. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and improve Klaroha.
  • Capture, qualify, route, and follow up on buyer intent according to customer instructions.
  • Create, review, schedule, publish, or analyze marketing assets when authorized by the customer.
  • Maintain audit logs, approval history, security controls, and integration health.
  • Provide support, troubleshoot issues, and communicate service updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

5. How we share information

We may share information with service providers who help us host, secure, support, and operate Klaroha; with connected platforms when a customer asks Klaroha to perform an action; with professional advisers where necessary; and with authorities when required by law.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share connected platform data for unrelated advertising or data brokerage purposes.

6. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect data, including access controls, encrypted credential storage where applicable, audit logging, and approval-gated workflows for external publishing actions.

No system is perfectly secure. Customers should use strong passwords, keep connected platform access limited to authorized personnel, and promptly revoke integrations that are no longer needed.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide Klaroha, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain audit logs, and enforce agreements. Customers may request deletion of workspace data or disconnection of integrations by contacting us.

When an integration is disconnected, Klaroha stops requesting new data from that platform. Data already stored in Klaroha may be deleted upon verified request, subject to legal, security, backup, and audit-retention requirements.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information.
  • Disconnect third-party integrations at any time where the product allows or by contacting us.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable law provides that right.
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing communications.

To make a request, email privacy@klaroha.com. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting on a request.

9. International processing

Klaroha and its service providers may process information in countries other than where you are located. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will provide notice by updating this page, notifying account administrators, or using another reasonable method.

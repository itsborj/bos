Brand essence

Noise enters.
Clarity exits.

Klaroha is demand-capture infrastructure for sales-led businesses. The visual identity should feel like a luxury performance system — dark, precise, composed, and engineered. The aesthetic reference is an Aston Martin DB12 V8 in Racing Green with satin bronze detailing.

Default surface
Ivory #F5F1E8 is the primary background for all marketing surfaces, landing pages, pitch decks, and print materials.
Dark accent
Obsidian #080B0D is used only for dark cockpit sections, product dashboards, footers, and high-contrast hero moments.
Apple principle
Clarity, deference, depth. Content leads. The interface recedes. Every element that does not improve clarity or confidence is removed.

Hard rule — Contrast & Readability
The foreground always reads against the background. Always.
Dark foreground on light background. Light foreground on dark background. No exceptions. This is not a preference — it is a hard rule that governs every text, label, icon, and UI element in the Klaroha system.

Light surface rule
All foreground elements must be drawn from the dark palette. Racing Green, Stone Deep, Obsidian, or Gold. Never Ivory, Champagne, or any light color on a light background.
Dark surface rule
All foreground elements must be drawn from the light palette. Ivory, Champagne, Muted Stone, Active Green, or Gold. Never Obsidian, Carbon, Graphite, or Racing Green on a dark background.
Additional violations
Gold on Racing Green background — both are mid-dark. Insufficient contrast for text or CTAs.
Stone on Obsidian body text — Stone (#A59674) is a label color only. Not readable at paragraph length on dark.
Gold as status indicator — Gold encodes premium / high-value. Never use it for live, error, or warning states.
Obsidian as default page background — Ivory is default. Obsidian is accent only for dark sections.
Champagne for body text — Too light on dark. Champagne (#D8BE8A) is metric numerals only.
Green fills on light cards — Racing Green is a text and border color on ivory, not a card fill outside CTA sections.

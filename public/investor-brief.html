02 — The Problem

Distributed Sales Organizations Leak Qualified Demand

The typical distributed sales organization has solved the wrong problem. It has invested in brand, advertising, and lead generation. It has not solved the operating layer between market attention and sales execution.

  • Serious buyers receive slow or no replies — inquiries scatter across Messenger, WhatsApp, email, forms, and phone
  • Sales time is wasted on unqualified leads because qualification depends on individual agent judgment
  • Follow-up is inconsistent — some agents call back in minutes, others wait days, most have no system
  • HQ cannot standardize local execution — top agent playbooks cannot be transferred to average performers
  • Performance is opaque — the organization knows spend but not which source, agent, or offer converts

Which channels are producing qualified conversations, which agents are converting them, and what should every agent do differently?

This pattern exists in every distributed sales organization in real estate, mortgage, franchise, insurance, and professional services. Existing tools — CRMs, automation platforms, messaging tools, agency lead generation, GoHighLevel — solve pieces of the problem without solving the operating layer.

03 — The Solution

Klaroha: The Operating Layer Between Market Attention and Sales Execution

Klaroha is not a CRM, not a marketing automation platform, and not an agency service. It installs knowing the customer's category — vertical playbooks, qualification flows, routing rules, and performance benchmarks are live from day one. The AI CMO holds the organization's brand, product, and operating context and is directed through natural language. Every output is subject to human approval before any action executes.

01
Intent Capture
Pages, funnels, forms, and inquiry assets across Google, Meta, WhatsApp, Messenger, listings, and partner networks — into a single qualified pipeline.
02
Qualification Flows
Category-specific mechanisms that determine buyer seriousness before sales time is spent. Installed with vertical playbooks for real estate, mortgage, franchise, and insurance.
03
Conversation Routing
Configurable routing from qualified inquiry to the right agent, branch, or specialist by product, geography, capacity, or business rules. Speed-to-lead monitored.
04
Follow-Up Engine
Automated and assisted follow-up — scripts, reminders, nurture sequences, reactivation workflows — tailored to the organization's category and offer.
05
Performance Visibility
Inquiry source, qualification rate, response time, agent and branch performance, conversion readiness. HQ sees the full picture; managers see their team; agents see their pipeline.
06
Vertical Playbooks
Category-specific templates, scripts, and benchmarks installed at setup. The system does not start blank. The organization's CMO is ready to operate from day one.

What Makes Klaroha Defensible

  • Vertical intelligence at setup — no months of configuration before the system reflects the category
  • Multi-brand, multi-market architecture — one command center for agencies, franchise networks, and multi-location operators with clean data separation
  • LLM-driven capability manifest — channels are installed as plugins; the platform scales capabilities without architectural complexity
  • BITE pipeline — every AI execution is tracked, reversible, and audit-trailed; critical for compliance-sensitive categories
  • Approval-gated execution — no campaign runs, message sends, or budget changes without an explicit human decision

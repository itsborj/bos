Business Model

Platform subscription plus usage

Base fees of $299–$1,499/month by organizational size, plus usage fees for AI module runs and active contacts.

Profitability & Margins
Gross margin 90%+ at steady state — the primary per-customer cost is a $20/month VPS, not labour or expensive LLM API.
AI Infrastructure
Customers on BYOK (their own API keys) pay platform fee only. Default platform-mode LLM cost is near zero, routed to free-tier providers (Gemini, Groq, open models) at early scale.

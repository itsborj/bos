The Ask

Funding Goals & Success Metrics

This raise is not for growth marketing. It is to complete the product to distribution-ready standard, sign the first ten paying distributed organizations, and validate the go-to-market in one English-speaking market.

Product Milestone
Vertical qualification flows, branch routing, and HQ performance dashboard shipped and stable for real estate or mortgage category.
Commercial Milestone
Ten paying organizations signed. At least three with 10+ agents or locations.
Go-To-Market Milestone
At least one category partnership or network agreement signed (franchise council, brokerage group, or lending network).
These milestones are achievable in twelve months and create the data needed to raise a seed round with demonstrated product-market fit.

Legal Structure & Operations Pool

Legal Structure
The cooperative's shareholding will be formalized through a multi-purpose cooperative entity registered in the Philippines, holding shares in the Klaroha operating company on behalf of its members. This structure is consistent with PH cooperative law and requires no unusual international legal mechanism.
Operations Pool (20%)
Allocated to team members contributing to engineering, product, sales, delivery, and support. Allocation is discretionary and milestone-based. This is equity distributed as part of compensation alongside market-rate pay, creating alignment beyond salary.

Supply-Side & Social Impact

Supply-Side Integration
Klaroha's vertical playbooks, qualification flows, and performance reporting are tools that PH marketing professionals can deploy, customize, and deliver as a service. The cooperative creates a ready-made implementation and support layer for Klaroha's target market without the cost of a direct field team.

Community & Social Purpose

The PH freelance economy is large, skilled, and systematically undervalued in the global marketing supply chain. Giving this community meaningful ownership in a platform that industrializes and elevates their contribution is both a mission statement and a talent magnet.

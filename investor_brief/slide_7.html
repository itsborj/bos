Segment 2: Fragmented High-Value Sellers

The Middle Layer (Beachhead to Enterprise)
Individual operators in high-value categories — real estate agents, mortgage advisors, insurance agents, vehicle sellers, clinic operators — who are part of larger distributed networks but make their own tool decisions. They adopt Klaroha to capture serious inquiries, qualify faster, and follow up before interest cools.

When enough practitioners in a brokerage or lending network adopt independently, the network HQ takes notice. This is the low-friction entry to enterprise without the enterprise sales cycle. Target ACV: $499–$699/month.

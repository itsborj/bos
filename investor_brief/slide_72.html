The Cooperative Advantage

A PH freelancer or agency that co-owns Klaroha has a financial incentive to deploy it for their clients. When Klaroha wins, the cooperative wins. It is a distributed GTM network embedded in the exact markets Klaroha targets.

The marketing freelancer who manages a franchise network's Google Ads and social accounts has more influence over that client's technology decisions than any sales representative. Cooperative ownership converts that influence into an aligned commercial relationship.

The Solution

Directing Intent, Not Tools

Intent-Based Interaction
The owner or sales director interacts with the system the way they would interact with a senior CMO: by giving intent, not by operating tools.

"Find out why our inquiry volume is up but our qualified conversation rate is down."
"Draft the follow-up sequence for our new townhouse release."
"Create a qualification checklist for the commercial loan inquiry form."

The CMO understands the business. It delegates execution to specialist modules. It produces artifacts — plans, drafts, qualification flows, performance reports — for human approval. It learns from what the organization approves and rejects.

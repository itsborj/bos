Business Model

  • Platform subscription tiered by organization size (Starter $299, Growth $699, Scale $1,499, Enterprise custom).
  • Usage‑based fees on AI runs, active contacts, and plugin executions.
  • High gross margin (>90%) – primary cost is a $20/month VPS per customer.
  • BYOK option for enterprise – customers bring their own LLM API keys, Klaroha charges platform fee only.
  • Revenue‑share on optional premium services (e.g., custom integrations, advanced analytics).

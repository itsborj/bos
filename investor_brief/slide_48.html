Go-To-Market

The Sales Motion

Founder-led Discovery → Product Demo → Pilot → Full Network Rollout

The Criticality of the Pilot
A well-designed 60-day pilot with a distributed sales team in a single market — with measurable outcomes including inquiry qualification rate, response time improvement, and qualified conversation volume — creates an ROI story that scales the network sale.
Scaling the Motion
For Enterprise and network contracts, a more structured account executive and solutions consultant motion is appropriate from year two onward.

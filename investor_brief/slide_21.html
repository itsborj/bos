The Problem

Why Existing Tools Fail

The tools that exist today solve pieces of the problem without solving the operating layer.

CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho)
Record-keeping systems, not demand-capture systems. They require agents to enter data (which most don't) and require complex manual builds for qualification and routing logic — expertise distributed organizations lack.
Marketing Automation (Klaviyo, etc.)
Audience-broadcast tools. They are not designed for real-time inquiry qualification or the complexities of branch-level routing.

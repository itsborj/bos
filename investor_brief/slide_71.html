The Strategic Role of the Cooperative

The 40% Cooperative Stake

The largest single stakeholder in Klaroha is not the founders or the investors. It is a multi-purpose cooperative of Philippines-based marketing freelancers and service providers. This is deliberate, and it is strategic.

The Globalized Marketing Engine
The Philippines has become one of the world's most significant contributors to outsourced digital marketing. PH-based freelancers and agencies manage social media, run Google and Meta campaigns, handle SEO, produce content, and operate lead generation systems for thousands of businesses across AU, UK, US, and other English-speaking markets.
Many of the distributed sales organizations in Klaroha's target market — real estate agencies, franchise networks, lending companies — already have a PH-based marketing support function, either directly or through outsourcing.

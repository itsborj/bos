Executive Summary

The Problem

The Revenue Gap
Distributed sales organizations lose qualified demand not from lack of market activity but from a broken operating layer. Inquiries scatter across Messenger, WhatsApp, email, forms, and phone. Agents reply inconsistently. Follow-up depends on individual discipline. HQ cannot see which channels produce qualified conversations or which playbooks convert.
Structural Inefficiency
One good agent or one good branch cannot transfer their execution to the rest of the network. The best organizations in high-value categories — real estate, mortgage, franchise, insurance, professional services — systematically lose revenue to this structural gap.

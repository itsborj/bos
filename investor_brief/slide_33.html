BITE Pipeline & Approval Gates

BITE Pipeline Architecture
The internal execution pipeline (Bridge–Intent–Transform–Evaluate) ensures that every marketing handoff is tracked, every AI execution is reversible with an audit trail, and no approved action happens without an explicit human decision. This is critical infrastructure for distributed organizations where compliance, brand consistency, and audit requirements are real.
Approval-Gated Execution
No AI module executes a campaign, sends a message, or changes an ad budget without human approval. The organization retains control; the system provides execution capacity. This is not a limitation — it is the design principle that makes the system trustworthy to an organization running multiple agents, branches, or client accounts.

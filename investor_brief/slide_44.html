Go-To-Market

Beachhead: AU & UK Markets

The initial go-to-market targets Australia and the United Kingdom as the beachhead for three strategic reasons.

1. High-Value Networks
AU and UK have mature real estate, mortgage brokerage, franchise, and insurance distribution networks. Organizations are commercially sophisticated and operate at price points where a $699–$1,499/month OS is a small fraction of potential revenue impact.
2. English-Speaking Advantage
Klaroha's LLM-driven CMO and qualification flows are built in English. Expanding here allows the product to reach scale before adding localization complexity. US expansion follows AU/UK market fit.
3. Founder Proximity & Network
Strategic access to key decision-makers in the AU/UK distributed sales ecosystem.

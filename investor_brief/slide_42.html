Why this model works
The platform fee provides predictable recurring revenue anchored to organizational commitment. Usage pricing aligns Klaroha's revenue to the organization's activity level — when campaigns run, when inquiries flow, when the AI CMO is actively working, usage fees accrue. An organization that is getting value from the system naturally generates more usage revenue.

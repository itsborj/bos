The Problem

High-Value Category Patterns (Cont.)

Franchise & Dealer
Multi-location consumer service brands, vehicle dealer groups, and equipment dealer networks all face the same HQ-versus-local tension: the brand is standardized; the sales execution is not.
Insurance Brokers
Distributed broker networks with multiple specialists. A prospect's inquiry may touch multiple specialists. Routing, qualification, and follow-up are almost entirely manual.
Professional Services
Accounting, legal, and specialist consulting practices. Inquiry capture is fragmented, qualification depends on the intake coordinator, and no one measures source performance.

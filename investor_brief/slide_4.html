Executive Summary

The Solution

Klaroha installs knowing the customer's category. A real estate brokerage network does not configure the system from scratch — it onboards to a platform that already understands buyer qualification, agent routing, and HQ visibility for real estate.

Day One Readiness
Vertical playbooks, qualification flows, follow-up scripts, performance dashboards, and a branch-and-agent routing engine are live from day one.
The AI CMO
The AI CMO holds the organization's brand, product, and operating context and can be directed through natural language to run campaigns, qualify prospects, surface performance gaps, and draft follow-up sequences — all subject to human approval before any action executes.

Diversified Acquisition Channels

Freelancer & Agency Community
A Klaroha deployment manages multiple client brands from one interface — exactly how a freelancer or agency operates. Community-led distribution through forums, networks (Upwork, LinkedIn), and agency owner networks builds adoption through practitioners who influence client tool decisions.
Practitioner-to-Network (Bottom-Up)
In real estate, mortgage, and insurance, the entry point is the individual agent — not the brokerage network. When practitioners adopt, network HQ takes notice. This bypasses the enterprise sales cycle, relying on product-led growth and peer recommendation.

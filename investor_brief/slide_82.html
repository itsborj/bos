Brand Identity & Expansion

Visual Identity
Klaroha is built as a premium brand for commercial buyers, referencing luxury performance (deep teal, obsidian, satin bronze, racing green). It is designed to feel credible in a procurement decision at a 50-location franchise network.

Category Expansion Roadmap

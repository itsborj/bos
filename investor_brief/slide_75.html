Ownership Structure

Investor Allocation

Investor Allocation: 30%

At pre-seed stage, this is the negotiating anchor. The founders' willingness to limit their own stake to 10% reflects conviction in the cooperative model and the operations pool as strategic assets, not personal enrichment goals.

The Strategic Difference
For investors, 30% of a company with a committed community distribution network, a ready-made supply-side channel, and an aligned operator pool is materially different from 30% of a conventional two-founder startup with the same cap table math.

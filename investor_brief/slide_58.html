LLM Cost Model (Platform Mode)

Klaroha's default package routes AI inference to free-tier providers (Gemini 2.0 Flash, Groq, OpenRouter). A shared local model handles lower-complexity tasks. This brings platform-mode LLM cost to near zero for the first 50–75 customers.

LLM Cost Stage Customers Monthly LLM Cost
Free tier sufficient 1–50 $0–50 (overflow only)
Partial paid routing 51–100 $150–400
Mixed free + paid 100+ $400–900
BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)
Customers who prefer premium models bring their own API keys. Klaroha earns no LLM margin on BYOK customers — revenue is platform fee only. This is a trust signal for enterprise buyers.

Go-To-Market

Channel Strategy: The Cooperative Engine

Cooperative Distribution

The PH-based marketing cooperative that co-owns 40% of Klaroha is the most direct initial channel. PH freelancers and marketing agencies already manage digital marketing, paid ads, content, and lead generation for thousands of AU, UK, and US clients.

Aligned Commercial Incentives
As co-owners with financial upside in Klaroha's growth, they have commercial incentive to deploy the platform for their clients rather than continuing to operate fragmented tool stacks. One cooperative member with eight clients represents eight potential deployments from a single aligned relationship. This channel requires no cold outreach — it is built into the ownership structure.

# Strategy Draft — Klaroha Dormant Prospect GTM Activation

Status: shadow prototype output  
Input: `01_buyer_map.md`  
Purpose: prove the lean workflow as a go-to-market strategy artifact, not an email campaign plan.

## Buyer map consumed

This strategy uses the dormant-prospect buyer map: problem-aware to solution-aware operators who already feel marketing execution is fragmented, but have not yet trusted Klaroha enough to restart a conversation. The dominant blockers are anxiety, habit, and low perceived likelihood. The trust gap is operational: Klaroha must prove it can make demand capture, approvals, follow-up, and performance visibility clearer without forcing a disruptive migration.

## GTM decision

The right go-to-market move is a dormant-prospect reactivation motion anchored on an operational-leakage diagnostic.

This is not an email campaign as the strategy. Email is only the first activation surface because the audience is dormant and already known. The GTM motion is to convert latent prior interest into qualified diagnostic conversations, learn which leakage points are most painful, then use those signals to decide which downstream artifacts and channels deserve investment.

## Market entry thesis

Klaroha should not try to win this segment by promising “more marketing output” first. Dormant prospects likely paused because the category already feels noisy, complex, or uncertain. The sharper entry point is:

> “Before adding another campaign, let’s find where your current marketing-to-pipeline loop is leaking.”

That lets Klaroha sell clarity before selling a platform. It also creates a practical first engagement that can produce buyer language, objections, and proof without asking the prospect to commit to a full operating-system rollout immediately.

## Positioning frame

For overloaded operators who already have marketing activity but still lack pipeline clarity, Klaroha is the operating loop that turns scattered signals, approvals, specialist work, and results into decisions the business leader can actually command.

Unlike a CRM, dashboard, agency retainer, or generic AI writing tool, Klaroha is organized around the full loop: context → specialist execution → human approval → measured outcome → better next move.

## Channel stack

### Do now

**Dormant-prospect Gmail reactivation**
Decision: first activation channel — buyer-map signal: problem-aware dormant prospects with anxiety and low perceived likelihood — proof required: a clear diagnostic offer and compliant prior-contact basis.
Rationale: Gmail is appropriate because the prospects are known and dormant, not anonymous cold traffic. The message must reopen the operational pain and invite a low-friction diagnostic, not pitch a broad platform demo.

**Diagnostic landing or one-page explainer**
Decision: conversion proof asset — buyer-map signal: trust gap around whether Klaroha can create order without adding complexity — proof required: a simple explanation of what the diagnostic inspects.
Rationale: A diagnostic page gives the email motion somewhere credible to point and reduces anxiety by making the next step concrete.

**Reply triage and qualification workflow**
Decision: pipeline control layer — buyer-map signal: habit and manual follow-up leakage — proof required: structured handling for interested, not-now, wrong-person, objection, and unsubscribe replies.
Rationale: The GTM motion fails if replies become another unmanaged inbox. Triage is part of the go-to-market system, not an admin afterthought.

### Hold

- Paid retargeting — hold until the reactivation motion identifies the strongest leakage pain and proof gap.
- Broad LinkedIn thought leadership — hold until the diagnostic produces sharper customer language.
- Newsletter nurture — hold until owned-list expectations and content cadence are confirmed.

### Avoid for now

- Generic “book a demo” blast — avoid because anxiety and low perceived likelihood make a broad demo ask too high-friction.
- Multi-channel launch calendar — avoid because the first strategic question is which leakage point converts dormant interest into conversation.
- Guaranteed ROI claims — avoid because no baseline proof or performance history was loaded.

## GTM sequence

### Stage 0 — Foundation

Confirm the dormant list is compliant, segmentable, and actually connected to prior Klaroha interest. Build the one-page diagnostic explanation and reply triage rules before any outreach. If this stage is skipped, the motion risks becoming an email blast instead of a controlled go-to-market test.

Completion signal: list basis confirmed, diagnostic offer written, reply categories defined, owner for replies assigned.

### Stage 1 — Reactivation test

Send a small controlled reactivation sequence through Gmail. The sequence should test whether dormant prospects respond more to follow-up leakage, approval chaos, reporting uncertainty, or content-execution drag. The goal is learning plus qualified conversations, not volume.

Completion signal: replies are categorized and the dominant pain language is visible.

### Stage 2 — Diagnostic conversion

Route interested replies into a diagnostic conversation or short intake. The diagnostic should map one leakage point in the prospect’s current marketing-to-pipeline loop and show how Klaroha would structure it.

Completion signal: qualified diagnostic conversations happen, objections are captured, and the clearest first-use case emerges.

### Stage 3 — Proof and expansion

Turn the strongest diagnostic patterns into proof assets: anonymized before/after workflow examples, objection responses, and a sharper page. Only after this stage should Klaroha expand into paid retargeting, broader content, or partner/community distribution.

Completion signal: proof asset exists and the next channel has a specific message grounded in real replies.

## First artifacts BOS should create

1. Diagnostic one-page explainer — defines the leakage audit and reduces implementation anxiety.
2. Gmail reactivation sequence — activates the known dormant audience with the diagnostic CTA.
3. Reply triage playbook — turns responses into qualified pipeline instead of inbox clutter.
4. Objection response sheet — handles CRM, agency, manual-process, and AI-tool objections.
5. Post-test learning brief — summarizes what pain language and conversion signals should shape the next GTM move.

## What this prototype is testing

This prototype tests whether one Strategy Draft can replace separate message-architecture and GTM-plan artifacts while keeping buyer-map rigor. The test passes only if a business leader can approve the GTM motion and a downstream specialist can create the next artifact without needing another strategy layer.

## Assumptions to validate

The dormant list must be compliant and tied to prior relationship or opt-in. If this is wrong, the GTM motion must switch from Gmail activation to consent-safe audience building or warm-network outreach.

The diagnostic must be a credible first offer. If prospects need to see the product before discussing leakage, the CTA should shift from diagnostic to short guided walkthrough.

Klaroha must be ready to handle replies quickly. If reply handling is slow or unclear, the strategy recreates the exact leakage problem it claims to solve.

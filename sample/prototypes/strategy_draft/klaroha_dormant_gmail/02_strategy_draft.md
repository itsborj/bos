# Strategy Draft — Klaroha

Request: Generate pipeline from dormant prospects through Gmail.  
Prototype path: buyer_map.v1 → Strategy Draft.  
Status: shadow prototype, not a production artifact.

## Buyer map consumed

This draft uses the dormant-prospect buyer map: problem-aware to solution-aware operators who already understand marketing execution is fragmented, but have not yet trusted Klaroha enough to restart a conversation. The primary blocking forces are anxiety, habit, and low perceived likelihood. The trust gap is not whether AI can generate marketing content; it is whether Klaroha can bring order, follow-up, and measurability to an operating environment that already feels complex.

## Strategic decision

Use Gmail as a reactivation and qualification path, not as a generic newsletter or cold outbound blast.

The goal is to turn dormant interest into qualified conversations by making the prospect feel: “This is still the same problem I had, Klaroha understands it, and the next step is small enough to take.” The sequence should avoid broad platform claims and instead anchor on a practical diagnostic: where demand signals, approvals, follow-up, or performance visibility are currently leaking.

## Message architecture

### Core message

Klaroha helps overloaded operators turn scattered marketing and sales signals into one clear operating loop, so demand does not get lost between tools, channels, approvals, and follow-up.

### Positioning frame

For sales-led or service-led businesses where marketing work is already happening across too many disconnected surfaces, Klaroha is a marketing operating system that makes demand capture, approval flow, and performance visibility easier to command.

Unlike another dashboard or a generic CRM, Klaroha is built around the decision loop: context in, specialist work out, human approval, result captured, next move improved.

### Proof arc

Before: the prospect has tools, people, and activity, but not enough clarity. Work is happening, but the business leader still asks what is working, what needs approval, and which prospects are falling through.

Trigger: the prospect realizes the issue is not only lead volume. It is leakage, slow decisions, and scattered execution.

Failed path: adding another tool, asking the team for more reports, hiring another specialist, or relying on manual follow-up without a shared operating loop.

Shift: Klaroha centralizes context, routes work through governed specialists, keeps the business leader in the approval seat, and turns outcomes into memory.

After: the business has a clearer pipeline from signal to approved action to measured learning.

### Objection responses

- “We already have a CRM.” Klaroha should be framed as the operating layer around marketing execution and demand capture, not as a CRM replacement.
- “This sounds like more complexity.” The first step should be a diagnostic of one leakage point, not a full implementation commitment.
- “Will my team use it?” The proof should show the review-and-approval loop, where humans stay in control and Klaroha reduces coordination work.

### Primary CTA

“Want me to map one leakage point in your current marketing-to-pipeline flow?”

### Secondary CTA

“If now is not the right time, reply with the one part of marketing that feels least clear right now: follow-up, approvals, reporting, or content execution.”

## Channel workflow

### Do now

**Gmail dormant-prospect sequence**
Decision: reactivation and qualification — buyer-map signal: anxiety + low perceived likelihood — proof required: a simple diagnostic or operating-loop example before asking for a demo.
Rationale: Dormant prospects already have some context, so the channel can be direct without feeling like a cold pitch. The sequence should reopen the pain, reduce perceived complexity, and invite a low-friction reply.

**Reply triage path**
Decision: conversion control — buyer-map signal: habit and anxiety — proof required: clear categories for replies and next-step routing.
Rationale: The value of the sequence is not just sent emails. It is whether replies become structured sales conversations instead of another inbox mess.

**One-page diagnostic offer**
Decision: proof and conversion asset — buyer-map signal: trust gap around operational clarity — proof required: a concrete example of what Klaroha will inspect.
Rationale: A low-commitment diagnostic is more believable than asking a dormant prospect to schedule a full demo immediately.

### Hold

- Broad newsletter — hold until a confirmed owned content cadence and audience expectation exists.
- Paid retargeting — hold until the dormant email sequence reveals the strongest pain language and proof gap.
- Generic demo campaign — hold until the diagnostic CTA has been tested against dormant prospects.

### Avoid for now

- Aggressive cold-sales language — avoid because anxiety is the dominant blocking force.
- “AI will replace your marketing team” framing — avoid because it increases adoption anxiety and violates Klaroha’s human approval posture.
- Guaranteed ROI claims — avoid because no baseline or proof was loaded.

## Gmail pipeline brief

The downstream Gmail specialist should create a short dormant-prospect sequence with four jobs:

1. Reopen the pain: remind the prospect of the operational drag Klaroha solves.
2. Show the shift: clarify that the first step is not a platform migration, but a leakage diagnostic.
3. Offer a low-friction reply: ask which part of the current marketing flow feels least clear.
4. Route intent: separate interested, not-now, wrong-person, and objection replies.

The sequence should not paste fake proof, invented metrics, or broad AI claims. It should sound like Klaroha: clear, practical, operator-facing, and focused on reducing noise.

## First workflow BOS should generate next

Create a Gmail dormant-prospect sequence artifact for review.

Required fields for that downstream artifact:

- sequence goal: reactivate dormant prospects into qualified diagnostic conversations
- target audience: prior Klaroha prospects with unresolved marketing-operation pain
- offer: one leakage-point diagnostic
- tone: clear, calm, operator-facing, non-hype
- CTA: reply with the least-clear part of their current marketing flow or book a diagnostic
- reply routing: interested / not now / wrong person / objection / unsubscribe

## Assumptions to validate

The first assumption is that Klaroha has permission to email these dormant prospects. If there is no opt-in, prior relationship, or compliant basis for outreach, Gmail execution must pause and the workflow should become a list-validation or consent-safe reactivation plan.

The second assumption is that dormant prospects remember enough about Klaroha for a direct reactivation note to feel relevant. If not, the first email needs more context and should not assume familiarity.

The third assumption is that the best first conversion is a diagnostic conversation, not a full product demo. If the business wants immediate demos, the CTA and qualification path should change.

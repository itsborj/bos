# Review Rubric — Lean Strategy Draft Prototype

Use this to compare the shadow prototype against the current GTM chain.

| Criterion | Pass signal | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer-map fidelity | Strategy decisions explicitly trace back to awareness, blocking forces, trust gap, and perceived likelihood. | 1–5 |
| GTM clarity | The terminal artifact is a go-to-market strategy, not an email plan or channel task. | 1–5 |
| Channel discipline | Gmail is treated as an activation surface inside the GTM motion, not the strategy itself. | 1–5 |
| Downstream usefulness | Next artifacts are clear enough for specialists to execute without another strategy layer. | 1–5 |
| Brand isolation | Klaroha only; no MotorMoto/Mactan context. | 1–5 |
| Proof discipline | No invented metrics, ROI, proof, or case studies. | 1–5 |
| Reviewability | Business leader can approve/revise in one pass. | 1–5 |
| Workflow efficiency | Fewer artifacts/calls while preserving buyer-map rigor. | 1–5 |

## Prototype hypothesis

The buyer map should remain mandatory, but the separate message-architecture and GTM-plan steps can be collapsed into one Strategy Draft that is still strong enough to brief downstream specialists.

# 02 — Strategy Draft

Brand: Klaroha  
Request: Generate pipeline from dormant prospects through Gmail.  
Prototype mode: buyer map → Strategy Draft. No separate message-architecture artifact. No separate GTM artifact.

## Buyer map consumed

This draft uses the dormant-prospect buyer map: problem-aware to solution-aware operators, early-majority buying behavior, anxiety and habit as the main blockers, and low perceived likelihood as the value-equation weakness.

The most important implication is that the strategy cannot be “send a campaign.” The buyer needs a low-risk reason to re-engage and a concrete demonstration that Klaroha reduces operational chaos instead of adding another system.

## GTM decision

Run a dormant-demand reactivation motion built around a one-leakage-point diagnostic.

Gmail is the first activation surface because the prospects are known and dormant. But the strategy is broader than email: convert prior interest into diagnostic conversations, identify which operating-leakage pain is most acute, then use those signals to decide the next channel and proof assets.

## Strategic thesis

Dormant Klaroha prospects are unlikely to restart because of a generic AI or software pitch. They are more likely to restart when the ask is small, concrete, and connected to their existing pain.

The best wedge is:

> “Before adding more marketing activity, find where your current marketing-to-pipeline loop is leaking.”

That offer respects the buyer map:

- It reduces anxiety because it is diagnostic, not a full migration.
- It works against habit because it does not attack the CRM, agency, or current process.
- It improves perceived likelihood because the first promise is specific: map one leakage point.

## Message architecture inside the draft

### Core message

Klaroha turns scattered marketing signals, approvals, specialist work, and performance feedback into one operating loop the business leader can command.

### Positioning frame

For overloaded operators who already have marketing activity but still lack pipeline clarity, Klaroha is the marketing operating layer that makes demand capture, approval flow, follow-up, and learning visible.

Unlike a CRM, dashboard, agency retainer, or generic AI writing tool, Klaroha is organized around the loop: context → specialist work → human approval → outcome memory → better next move.

### Proof arc

Before: the business has activity but not clarity. Campaigns, follow-up, approvals, and reporting live in different places.

Trigger: the prospect realizes the issue is not only lead volume; it is leakage and coordination drag.

Failed path: another dashboard, another manual report, another specialist handoff, or another CRM tweak.

Shift: Klaroha maps the operating loop and shows where demand is being lost or slowed.

After: the business leader can see what needs action, what is waiting for approval, and what should improve next.

### Objection responses

- “We already have a CRM.” Klaroha does not need to replace the CRM. It can operate around the marketing-to-pipeline loop and expose what the CRM does not make clear.
- “This sounds complicated.” The first step is one leakage diagnostic, not a platform migration.
- “We already have an agency.” Klaroha can make agency work, approvals, and outcomes more visible instead of replacing the agency by default.

### CTA

Primary: “Want me to map one leakage point in your current marketing-to-pipeline flow?”

Secondary: “If now is not the time, reply with the part that feels least clear: follow-up, approvals, reporting, or execution.”

## Channel stack

### Do now

**Dormant-prospect Gmail reactivation**
Role: first activation surface.
Why: known dormant prospects can be reactivated with a focused diagnostic ask. The message should not behave like cold outbound or a newsletter.
Proof needed: compliant list basis and a clear diagnostic offer.

**Diagnostic one-page explainer**
Role: conversion proof asset.
Why: reduces anxiety by showing what the diagnostic inspects before asking for a conversation.
Proof needed: simple example of a leakage map or operating-loop diagram.

**Reply triage workflow**
Role: pipeline control.
Why: if replies become unmanaged inbox clutter, Klaroha recreates the leakage problem it claims to solve.
Proof needed: clear routing for interested, not now, wrong person, objection, unsubscribe.

### Hold

- Paid retargeting — hold until the reactivation test identifies the strongest pain language.
- Broad LinkedIn content — hold until diagnostic conversations produce sharper proof and objections.
- Newsletter nurture — hold until owned-list expectations and cadence are confirmed.

### Avoid for now

- Generic “book a demo” blast — too high-friction for anxiety + low perceived likelihood.
- Multi-channel launch calendar — premature before knowing which leakage pain reactivates demand.
- Guaranteed ROI claims — no baseline or proof loaded.

## GTM sequence

### Stage 0 — Foundation

Confirm the dormant list is compliant and tied to prior relationship or opt-in. Build the diagnostic one-pager and reply triage rules. This prevents the motion from becoming a generic email campaign.

Completion signal: list basis confirmed, diagnostic page drafted, reply categories assigned.

### Stage 1 — Reactivation test

Send a controlled Gmail sequence to dormant prospects. Test which pain reopens the conversation: follow-up leakage, approval drag, unclear reporting, or execution noise.

Completion signal: replies categorized and dominant pain language identified.

### Stage 2 — Diagnostic conversion

Move interested replies into a short diagnostic conversation. The diagnostic maps one leakage point and recommends the next operating-loop improvement.

Completion signal: qualified conversations completed and objections captured.

### Stage 3 — Proof-building and channel expansion

Turn the best diagnostic patterns into proof assets: anonymized before/after workflow, objection handling, and stronger landing copy. Then decide whether paid retargeting, LinkedIn, partner outreach, or content expansion is justified.

Completion signal: proof asset created and next channel chosen from real response data.

## Assumptions to validate

The dormant list must be compliant. If not, the motion shifts to consent-safe reactivation or warm-network outreach.

The diagnostic offer must feel useful without requiring a full product demo. If prospects ask to see the product first, the CTA should shift to a guided walkthrough.

Klaroha must be ready to respond quickly. If replies sit unmanaged, the GTM motion fails its own promise.

## Approval note

This is a draft go-to-market strategy. It does not send email, publish content, spend budget, or mutate external systems. Execution requires separate approval.

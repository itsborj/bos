# 03 — Downstream Work Orders From Strategy Draft

Purpose: prove the Strategy Draft can drive execution without a separate message architecture or GTM artifact.

These are not Stream artifacts. They are work orders a future orchestrator could dispatch after the Strategy Draft is approved.

## Work order 1 — Diagnostic one-page explainer

### Job

Explain the one-leakage-point diagnostic so dormant prospects understand the next step before booking or replying.

### Must include

- What Klaroha inspects: signals, approvals, follow-up, reporting, execution handoffs.
- What the prospect gets: one mapped leakage point and a recommended next move.
- What it is not: not a full migration, not a CRM replacement, not an automated send.

### Source from Strategy Draft

- GTM decision: dormant-demand reactivation around a leakage diagnostic.
- Trust gap: prove operational clarity.
- CTA: map one leakage point.

## Work order 2 — Dormant-prospect Gmail sequence

### Job

Reactivate prior Klaroha interest into diagnostic replies or bookings.

### Sequence jobs

1. Reopen the operational pain.
2. Show the diagnostic as a low-risk next step.
3. Address CRM/agency/manual-process objections.
4. Close the loop with a graceful reply option.

### Must not include

- fake ROI
- fake case studies
- aggressive urgency
- “AI replaces your team” language
- send automation promises

### Source from Strategy Draft

- Blocking forces: anxiety, habit, low perceived likelihood.
- CTA: reply with least-clear area or request diagnostic.
- Voice: clear, practical, operator-facing.

## Work order 3 — Reply triage playbook

### Job

Prevent replies from becoming unmanaged inbox clutter.

### Categories

- Interested: send booking link or ask one scoping question.
- Not now: ask for follow-up timing.
- Wrong person: request correct contact.
- Objection: answer with operating-layer distinction.
- Unsubscribe: suppress immediately.

### Source from Strategy Draft

- Channel stack: reply triage workflow is a Do-now part of the GTM motion.

## Work order 4 — Objection response sheet

### Job

Prepare concise answers for predictable dormant-prospect objections.

### Objections

- We already have a CRM.
- We already have an agency.
- This sounds complicated.
- AI tools have not worked for us.
- We do this manually.

### Source from Strategy Draft

- Message architecture section: objection responses.

## Work order 5 — Post-test learning brief

### Job

Summarize what the reactivation test revealed before expanding channels.

### Must answer

- Which pain got the most replies?
- Which objection appeared most often?
- Did prospects prefer diagnostic, walkthrough, or later follow-up?
- What proof asset is needed before the next channel?

### Source from Strategy Draft

- Stage 3: proof-building and channel expansion.

The Cooperative · 40%
The channel is built into
the ownership structure.
Distribution alignment. A PH freelancer or agency that co-owns Klaroha has a financial incentive to deploy it for their clients. When Klaroha wins, the cooperative wins.
Credibility at the point of delivery. The marketing freelancer managing a franchise network's Google Ads has more influence over that client's technology decisions than any sales rep Klaroha could hire.
Supply-side integration. The cooperative creates a ready-made implementation and support layer for Klaroha's target market — without the cost of a direct field team.
Social purpose. Giving the PH freelance economy meaningful ownership in a platform that elevates their contribution is both a mission statement and a talent magnet.
For investors
30% of a company with a committed community distribution network, a ready-made supply-side channel, and an aligned operator pool is materially different from 30% of a conventional two-founder startup.

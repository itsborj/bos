Six ways organizations
lose serious buyers
01
Slow or no reply
Inquiries scatter across Messenger, WhatsApp, email, and forms. No one owns response speed.
02
Wasted sales time
Qualification depends on each agent's individual judgment. Low-quality leads consume the same effort as serious ones.
03
Inconsistent follow-up
Some agents call back in minutes. Others wait days. Most have no system. Interest goes cold.
04
HQ cannot standardize execution
Every branch runs a different playbook. Top performers cannot transfer their method to the rest of the network.
05
Performance is opaque
The organization knows spend. It does not know which source produced a qualified conversation, or which agent converted it.

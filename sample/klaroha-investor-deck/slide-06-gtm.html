PH-based marketing professionals already operate inside the organizations Klaroha targets. Co-ownership aligns their commercial incentive with Klaroha's growth.
Primary Channel · Now
Cooperative Distribution
The 40%-owner PH cooperative manages digital marketing for thousands of AU, UK, and US clients. As co-owners, they have financial incentive to deploy Klaroha rather than operate fragmented tool stacks. One cooperative member with eight clients = eight potential deployments from a single aligned relationship.
No cold outreach. The channel is built into the ownership structure.
Secondary Channel · Active
Freelancer & Agency Community
Beyond the cooperative, the broader English-speaking freelancer and digital marketing agency market is a natural fit. A Klaroha deployment manages multiple client brands from one interface — exactly how a freelancer or agency operates. Community-led distribution through digital marketing forums, LinkedIn communities, and agency owner networks.
Practitioners influence their clients' tool decisions.
Third Motion · Building
Practitioner-to-Network
For real estate, mortgage, and insurance categories, the entry point is the individual agent — not the brokerage network. When practitioners adopt, network HQ takes notice. This channel requires no enterprise sales cycle at the start. It requires a product compelling enough that practitioners recommend it upward.
Bottom-up enterprise without the enterprise sales cycle.

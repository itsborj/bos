Card Variants

Five elevation and treatment levels for different informational weights. All support hover lift.

📈
Default Surface
Border + subtle shadow. The baseline for most content cards in list and grid layouts.
border + shadow-sm elevation 1
💡
Elevated Surface
No border, medium shadow. For focus items, tooltips, or modals that need to float.
shadow-md elevation 2
🗂️
Outlined Surface
Strong border, no shadow. Use for grouped content or clear structural delineation.
border 1.5 elevation 0
✦
Warm Surface

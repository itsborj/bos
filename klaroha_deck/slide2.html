The Company

  • Captures buyer intent across channels (Messenger, WhatsApp, email, forms, phone)
  • Qualifies leads, routes them to the right agent or branch, and surfaces performance to HQ
  • AI‑powered CMO‑in‑pocket: users give intent, the system produces work, humans approve
  • Replaces a marketing department, CRM, lead‑routing tool, follow‑up engine, and reporting stack

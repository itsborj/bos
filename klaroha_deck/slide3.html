The Problem

  • Qualified demand leaks – not a lack of activity but a broken operating layer.

  • Agents reply inconsistently; follow‑up depends on individual discipline.
  • HQ cannot see which channels produce qualified conversations or which playbooks convert.
  • High‑performer knowledge does not flow to the rest of the network.

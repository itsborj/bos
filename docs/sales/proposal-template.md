## 2. What we're proposing

A managed production operation for [Brand], running on the Klaroha Business Operating System.

**What that means in practice:**

- **Your brand, locked at the system level.** Your logo, colors, voice, and visual constraints are loaded into the system once, during onboarding — then enforced on every production automatically. The system cannot produce off-brand output.
- **Approval-gated production.** Every piece moves through checkpoints you control: you read the full production blueprint before anything renders, preview motion before the final cut, and correct static ad drafts at zero cost before any image generates. Nothing expensive runs without your sign-off.
- **One review desk.** Every deliverable arrives in one feed, previewed exactly as it will appear on the platform. Approve, revise (with attached references), or publish — in place.
- **Campaign memory.** Your approvals, corrections, and rejections are recorded and carried forward. You brief once. You never re-explain a correction. The operation survives any staffing change — including ours.
- **A dedicated operator.** A human managing your pipeline: brief intake, production oversight, approval coordination, delivery to your Drive.

**Every asset is delivered to your own Google Drive. You own the output the moment it exists.**

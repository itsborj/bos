## 3. Video Production — $2 per second of rendered video

Video is produced through the same staged process real studios use:

```
Concept → Storyboard → Approval → Render → Final review → Publish
```

**Concepting and storyboards are part of your monthly plan.** Iterating on the idea, the script, the shot list, and the storyboard costs you nothing — this is where we refine until it's right.

**Rendering is billed at $2 per second of produced video, per render.** No render ever starts without your approval of the storyboard first. You see exactly what the video will be — and exactly what it will cost — before a single second is billed.

**Example:** a 15-second social cut = **$30 per render pass**.

### How revisions work

Changes requested **before** rendering (at the storyboard stage) are free and unlimited.

Changes requested **after** a render are produced as a new render and billed at the same per-second rate. This is why we invest heavily in the storyboard stage — it's where iteration is free, and it's how we keep your production spend low and predictable.

Video production is billed monthly in arrears, itemized per video and per render.
